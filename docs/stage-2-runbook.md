- Different structured start instants withhold the event interval; identical duplicate starts remain valid.
- Conditional recurrence exceptions prevent automatic occurrence expansion or silent removal of a date.
- A stated weekday conflicting with a source date withholds the instant and retains the source date for review.
- Different location claims across separate performances leave their locations unresolved instead of assigning one venue to every performance.
