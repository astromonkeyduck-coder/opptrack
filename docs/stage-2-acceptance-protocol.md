# Stage 2 acceptance protocol and custody tools

`evaluation/stage2-acceptance-protocol-v1.json` records the owner's **six confirmed source-representation rules** and remains pending candidate freeze plus independent holdout family/exposure/annotation work. It changes no old label, scorer or gate. The working target is near100 directly human-labeled independent holdout examples, with at least95% correct event recall and95% correct action recall, no unsupported critical facts or invented event/action units, exhaustive entity/field review and explicit unknowns. The pack's roughly100 combined wording and the repository's provisional40 minimum remain recorded separately; the owner confirmed the stricter near100 holdout target, and forty alone is not presented as satisfying the near100 holdout requirement.

Source-observation extraction, human-resolved canonical occurrences/milestones and reconciliation have separate denominators. Human annotations preserve literal source date, event-local date and UTC instant; partial dates/clocks and ranges must not be repaired into invented years, days, instants or performances. Publishing-live/absent-STATUS handling and canonical association now follow the confirmed representation rules. The record does not approve future case-specific family assignments, factual gold or remaining posted-versus-open ambiguity. Structural JSON validity and an exact quote do not prove semantic truth.

The proposed native-v5/native-media-v2 candidate is deterministic, with no model inference or inference prompt. Its quality selection remains unaccepted. The earlier schema-first/evidence-first prompts on the local0.5B model are disqualified **development-only** evidence: twenty authorized generations were consumed, zero outputs validated, and no further inference or production model integration follows.

`evaluation/stage2-acceptance-guards.ts` validates manual annotation structure, explicit null reasons, exact text/visual evidence references, source/canonical associations, owner/exposure bindings and prediction seals. `evaluation/stage2-acceptance-files.ts` provides the real filesystem `wx` receipt implementation. `withBlindedGold` is the one-use scoring boundary; the final scorer callback is bound by `evaluation/stage2-acceptance-runner.ts`, validates the sealed result body and original source/page custody before gold, and validates human annotations against the source-only frozen views inside the one-use callback. No real holdout score or real gold access has run through these new tools.

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/stage2-acceptance-tool.ts freeze TEMPLATE.json evaluation/NEW-FREEZE.json
node --import tsx scripts/stage2-acceptance-tool.ts check-freeze evaluation/NEW-FREEZE.json
node --import tsx scripts/stage2-acceptance-tool.ts bind-confirmed-rules evaluation/NEW-FREEZE.json private/stage2-acceptance-human-review/RULE-BINDING.json
node --import tsx scripts/stage2-acceptance-tool.ts check-pre-gold FREEZE.json OWNER-REVIEW.json EXPOSURE-REVIEW.json SEALED-PREDICTIONS.json

```

The last command is for the independent annotation/curator process after freeze. It prints counts/structural errors without source facts or answers and performs no scoring. The commands do not infer, provision, contact sources, read dotenv/credential files or run a scorer implicitly. Freeze outputs are exclusive writes; existing freezes are never overwritten. Environment metadata uses a closed nonsecret schema, with model/calendar/email effects false. Do not put tokens, cookies or credentials in a metadata template.

Human annotation and prediction files belong in ignored `private/stage2-acceptance-human-review/` and `private/stage2-acceptance-predictions/`. Receipts belong in ignored `private/stage2-acceptance-runs/<source-custody-sha256>/`. Real labels remain absent until direct human review. Keep sealed source contents unavailable to the runtime engineer during candidate development; a source-count/hash report is not proof that family independence has been human checked.

The new guard tests use synthetic source text, synthetic labels, synthetic approvals and temporary filesystem receipts only. They neither open sealed real source contents/answers nor create a real freeze, assignment, prediction, approval or holdout result.


The supplied `evaluation/stage2-acceptance-freeze-template-v1.json` lists actual native-v5, native-media-v2, source-only-text-v3 and transitive repository dependencies, plus the confirmed rule-record hash binding. Its local native/Node versions were read with version-only commands; they are not a Cloud-environment claim. The final acceptance matcher/scorer and source-only native runner now have synthetic tests and explicit artifact bindings, so `finalScorerMatcherBindingReady=true`. It remains `templateOnly=true`: verify the intended environment and deliberately set that flag false before creating a real freeze. No actual freeze, real prediction or holdout score was created here.

The six-rule binding command checks the exact original confirmation bytes and reviewed-sheet SHA before producing a new immutable freeze-bound sidecar. It preserves the recorded receipt time and unknown exact review time. The sidecar's scope is only those six representation rules; it cannot create an independent family/exposure review or factual label approval.


The final matcher requires the same case/source units/kind and mutually unique native, action, time or literal-title identity. Contradictory stated identities cannot fall back to a title match. Ambiguous entities remain misses and extras. The scorer reports source observations and human canonical units separately; canonical units cannot be manufactured by combining incomplete source predictions. It reports every labeled nonnull field, wrong/unsupported facts, null abstentions, known omissions, unscored fields/entities, per-kind correct-unit recall, evidence/source/page failures, explicit negatives and wrong merges/retained duplicates/unresolved/unlabeled relation pairs. Unscored extras block the gate. ISO comparison requires explicit offsets; no missing civil date, zone or date-range conversion is inferred. The sole field-name projection is the frozen `price` to `cost` alias, and collisions fail.

Prediction reads hash-verified original source bytes and the frozen text-v3/native-media-v2 pipeline, never human labels. Every failed job remains an explicit failed result. Expected blocked challenges are separate from unexpected failures and earn no semantic-negative credit. The source-only reconciliation adapter calls the unchanged production suggestion module, using literal frozen providers, exact source-record/evidence bindings, explicit native opportunity links or original HQ RSVP links and verified equal intervals. Feed/index URLs are never authority fallbacks. Unknown status stays unknown. It emits positive suggestions only; absent authority leaves a visible abstention. A human-reviewed negative pair with no positive suggestion is evaluated as keeping those units separate, rather than claiming semantic different. Missing positive duplicate suggestions remain retained duplicates; unresolved truth and predictions stay separately reported. The scorer freezes its binding, verifies original assets and all retained PDF page views, validates the complete prediction roster before the exclusive claim, and opens gold only afterward. The receipt records success/failure and cannot be reused under another candidate name. Passing extraction holdout gates alone still returns `stage2Accepted=false`; it does not waive media/social-access gaps or the carried Stage1 requirements.

The frozen critical-field registry prevents partial dates/clocks/ranges, precision/phase, status, availability, cost, eligibility, venue/building, organizer, identity and routing facts from being marked noncritical to evade the critical-fact rule. The fixed zero-error gate applies to critical wrong/unsupported facts. Noncritical errors and all-field precision remain reported, and every nonnull output still needs a human label. Positive negative/reconciliation strata prevent vacuous passes. The original criteria and old scorers remain unchanged.

Before direct annotation, the independent curator can export authenticated original/transformed source views without running extraction or opening labels:

```sh

```

Source views preserve omitted original text facts, so a lossy transform does not narrow the human entity/field scope. Raw and transformed offsets require their own exact hashes; they cannot stand in for each other. Known retained facts need evidence in the actual representation used by the extractor as well as the original source review. The annotation checker recomputes these views from verified custody rather than trusting an arbitrary supplied view file. Curator and scorer outputs suppress JSON parse/schema snippets; resolved credential paths and source symlinks escaping custody are rejected before bytes are read.
