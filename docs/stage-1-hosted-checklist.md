
Optional audit-only values may be entered directly in ignored `.env.hosted-check.local`: NETLIFY_AUTH_TOKEN, TRIGGER_PRODUCTION_READ_KEY, HOSTED_CHECK_OWNER_ACCESS_TOKEN and HOSTED_CHECK_OTHER_ACCESS_TOKEN.
